Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 Reference Guide

A practical summary of statutory packaging rules, font sizes, units of measurement, and penal clauses under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.

Rule 6: Mandatory Declarations on Every Package

  • Rule 6(1)(a): Common or generic name of commodity.
  • Rule 6(1)(b): Net quantity in standard SI metric units (g, kg, ml, l, N).
  • Rule 6(1)(c): Month and year of manufacture, packing, or import.
  • Rule 6(1)(d): Name and complete address of manufacturer, packer, or importer with PIN code.
  • Rule 6(1)(e): Maximum Retail Price (MRP) with explicit phrase "inclusive of all taxes".
  • Rule 6(1)(f): Unit Sale Price (USP) for packages containing more than 1kg or 1L.
  • Rule 6(1)(n): Consumer Care helpline telephone number and email address.
  • Rule 6(10): Country of Origin for imported commodities.

Section 36: Penalty for Non-Compliance

Any person who manufactures, packs, imports, sells, distributes, or exposes for sale any packaged commodity which does not conform to the declarations on the package shall be punished with:

First Offence: Fine which may extend up to ₹25,000.
Second Offence: Fine which may extend up to ₹50,000.
Subsequent Offences: Fine of ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000, OR imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year, or both.

Rule 11 & 12: Standard Units of Measurement

Packages must only use standard metric units. Prohibited non-standard forms include:

Quantity Permitted (Legal) Prohibited (Illegal)
Mass (Weight) g, kg, mg gm, gms, gms., kgs
Volume (Liquid) ml, l, L, mL ml., ltr, ltrs, mls
Count / Number N, U, Units, count ct., pcs. (ambiguous)

Unit Sale Price (USP) Amendment Mandate

Under the latest amendments to the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules:

  • Packages with net quantity greater than 1 kg or 1 litre must declare USP per gram (₹/g) or per millilitre (₹/ml).
  • Multi-piece packaged items must declare USP per number / unit (₹/N).
  • USP must be rounded off to the nearest two decimal places and printed adjacent to MRP in equal prominence.
